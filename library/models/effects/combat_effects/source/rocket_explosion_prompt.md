# Rocket explosion atlas

Created with the built-in image-generation tool. The PNG is delivered
pixel-for-pixel by `../export_fireball.py`.

Prompt: create a dedicated rocket-impact 4-by-4 transparent RGBA animation atlas,
sixteen chronological frames, read left-to-right and top-to-bottom. A hot
white-yellow warhead core swells rapidly into a broad asymmetric rolling orange
fireball with a thick charcoal soot mantle and large separated curled lobes.
Give it a slightly diagonal upper-left punch and lower-right rolling soot skirt;
do not add a long jet. Frames one through three expand rapidly, four through
seven sustain a rich orange interior behind dark smoke, eight through ten retain
receding hot pockets and sparks, and eleven through sixteen cool into neutral
slate-grey soot and faint wisps. Keep all sixteen cells nonempty. Distinguish it
from a tiny grenade flash and a flamethrower stream. No beige dust, uniform glowing
veins, mushroom stalk, ground plane, text, grid, checkerboard or cast shadows.
All content, including glow and detached sparks, fits in the central half
of each equal cell with 25 percent fully transparent margins on every side.
Scale and recenter complete sprites without cropping. Preserve alpha.

# Cryo pod

Static environment prop. Editable geometry is generated by `model.py` in headless
Blender 5.2; exports and previews are ignored build outputs.

## Scale and orientation

Metres; floor-centred origin at z = 0. The long axis is Y, the occupant's
head is towards -Y, and the canopy/opening faces +Z (Godot +Y after GLB export).
Overall envelope, including retained fittings: **1.065 x 2.525 x 1.147 m**
(width x length x height). The thermal berth is **0.70 x 2.02 m**, at z =
**0.460 m**. Nominal length allowance over the 1.753 m Tensor is 267 mm.

`inspection.py` also checks the actual generated Tensor, rigidly reclined with
his arms lowered 85 degrees within the authored joint limits. The resulting
patient is 0.597 m wide, 1.727 m long and 0.440 m deep. It has no intersections
with the finished pod and at least **36.9 mm** non-deck clearance. The bedside
comparison uses Tensor's authored idle; the occupant view uses a static
inspection pose. Tensor and its actions are never saved or exported in the pod.

## Construction

The asset has a horizontal pressure capsule, with a head-side glass
canopy and foot-side insulated metal hood. Both halves follow one continuous
hollow loft and meet through a pressure joint, rather than overlapping shells.
The tub is a carved lofted monocoque with a continuous gasket, retained frame,
matching service pockets, supported berth and head pad. Two bored clevis hinges
have coaxial retained pins; opposite compression latches secure the closed lid.
This is the sealed static state; no opening sweep or actuator is authored.

Each visible fitting has a purpose:

- The bolted hood hatch gives access to the foot-side diagnostic/service bay.
- Four flush side cassettes cover thermal-service channels, with recessed vents.
- Two rear pressure-service collars retain recessed plugs and valve hexes; the
  central bulkhead grille is the purge cartridge.
- The skid supports two broad saddle castings and the coolant-plant spine.
- Stainless return tubes terminate on real underside ports, and bored saddle
  clamps retain their straight runs. The signal loom terminates on two cabinet
  strain reliefs and stays clear of both supports and the skid.
- Mirrored side telemetry cassettes expose the original library service UI,
  with analytic screen UVs preserved separately from the painting atlas.

Glazing uses `stasis_chamber`'s cyan material: **18% opacity**, IOR **1.46**,
roughness **0.16**, separate from the opaque mesh. The canopy is a hollow
formed double wall, 12 mm across its straight sides and 14 mm at the crown.
The signal loom uses the same constant-radius, tangent-elbow conduit helper as
`workstation`; coolant tubes use the stasis chamber's closed mitred sweep.
Off-white and grey paint, matte black structure, stainless steel and bronze
bearing/valve details retain the library's manufactured prop language.

## Regeneration

Run from `library/`, using the Blender 5.2 executable in `../docs/library/MODELLING.md`:

```powershell
& $blender --background --factory-startup --python models/environments/cryo_pod/model.py
& $blender --background --factory-startup --python models/environments/cryo_pod/preview.py
& $blender --background --factory-startup --python models/environments/cryo_pod/inspection.py
& $blender --background --factory-startup --python models/environments/cryo_pod/collision.py
```

`inspection.py -- --fit-only` checks the actual occupant without rendering.
Standard previews are unchanged library studio views. Additional inspection
images are `hero`, `scale`, `occupied` and `occupied_top`. Inspection never
saves the loaded scene.

## Verification

Regenerate from `model.py`, run the asset validators and collision checks,
perform fresh GLB/FBX round trips, and inspect the native previews.
The generated rig metadata records delivered dimensions, budgets,
materials, attachment frames and animation clips.
